# Interview Template

A template for character-driven interview narratives with documentary footage.

## Structure

This template is designed for:
- Personal interviews with animated characters
- B-roll footage interspersed with narration
- Multi-scene progression through the interview

## Scenes

1. **Introduction** - Sets the context, introduces the subject
2. **Main Interview** - Core interview content with supporting footage
3. **Conclusion** - Wraps up with final thoughts and closing video

## Assets Needed

### Models
- `environment.glb` - 3D environment/room
- `interviewer.glb` - Optional interviewer character
- `interview_subject.glb` - Main interview subject character

### Audio
- `intro_narration.mp3` - Introduction narration
- `interview.mp3` - Main interview audio
- `conclusion.mp3` - Concluding narration

### Video
- `introduction.mp4` - Opening video/context
- `broll_1.mp4` - Supporting footage 1
- `broll_2.mp4` - Supporting footage 2
- `closing.mp4` - Closing video

### Images
- `background.jpg` - Home screen background
- `logo.png` - Organization logo

## Customization

1. Replace placeholder assets with your content
2. Update `story.json` with correct timing (`triggerAt` values)
3. Adjust character positions based on your environment
4. Customize UI text for your audience

## Best Practices

- Keep audio clips under 3-4 minutes per scene
- Position video projections where users naturally look
- Use B-roll to illustrate key points
- Test timing with actual content before deployment
