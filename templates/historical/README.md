# Historical Reconstruction Template

A template for timeline-based historical storytelling using archival footage and 3D reconstructions.

## Structure

This template is designed for:
- Timeline-based historical narratives
- Archival footage integration
- 3D artifact displays
- Walking positional audio tours

## Scenes

1. **Era 1 (1920s)** - Sets historical context with early footage
2. **Era 2 (1940s)** - Conflict period with moving audio narration
3. **Era 3 (1960s)** - Transition period
4. **Present** - Modern reflection with multi-wall video

## Key Features

### Positional Audio
The template demonstrates moving audio narration that guides users through the space. Waypoints define the audio source position over time.

### Multi-Wall Video
The final scene shows how to display video across multiple walls simultaneously for an immersive conclusion.

### Artifact Display
Static 3D objects represent historical artifacts that users can examine.

## Customization

1. Adjust era names and dates for your story
2. Position waypoints based on your environment layout
3. Coordinate video triggers with narration timing
4. Customize lighting per era for mood (dimmer = older, brighter = modern)

## Best Practices

- Use sepia/vintage filters on archival footage
- Keep artifacts at eye level for examination
- Use positional audio to guide exploration
- Transition lighting gradually between eras
- Multi-wall video works best in contained spaces
